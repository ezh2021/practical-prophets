About this edition

Practical Prophets is a narrative nonfiction adaptation of the Masoretic Hebrew Bible. It is not a line-by-line translation. Cross-references and Jewish interpretations are distinguished from the original account, and the two voices mark story and commentary.

Editorial note: These are working narrated editions. Independent verse-by-verse review and Hebrew pronunciation improvements remain in progress. Judges includes sexual violence, forced marriage, killing and civil war; these passages are told without invented scenes.
